JUJUTSU TRIALS — FULL CHANGELOG
================================

This changelog summarizes the changes discussed throughout the game-development conversation. The VR interactions and visual effects still need in-game testing.

1. ORIGINAL GAME AND CORE SYSTEMS
---------------------------------
- Built the game as a standalone HTML project using Three.js and WebXR.
- Added VR hand tracking and gesture-based technique activation.
- Added cursed energy, health, technique costs, cooldowns, enemy encounters, and combat effects.
- Included character/domain selection and multiple difficulty modes.
- Included Training Mode and an option to quit training.
- Added enemy grades, escalating encounters, and boss fights.
- Included Gojo, Sukuna, Megumi, Todo, Jogo, and Hakari.

2. HAKARI — MOVES AND DOMAIN
----------------------------
Ranged moves:
- Changed Sliding Doors to adapt its range to the nearest living enemy in Hakari's forward lane.
- Added a fallback range when no suitable target is found.
- Added target-lock status and range feedback.
- Extended Rush into a ranged wave with a reported 14-metre range.
- Extended Shoulder Charge into a ranged shockwave with a reported 11-metre range.

Move visuals:
- Improved Sliding Doors with a door frame, glowing panels, and an opening animation.
- Added layered energy waves and impact effects to Rush.
- Added an animated 3D shockwave and impact effects to Shoulder Charge.

Domain Expansion:
- Added a full-scene, neon casino and railway-inspired environment.
- Added animated jackpot signage, glowing tracks, rotating rings, and floating particles.
- Connected Hakari's jackpot activation to the domain environment.

Note: The adaptive range logic exists in the source, but whether it selects and hits targets correctly needs in-game testing.

3. TODO — BOOGIE WOOGIE
-----------------------
- Replaced the clap gesture with bringing both fists together.
- Updated gesture instructions and control messages.
- Kept Boogie Woogie as a separate ability with cursed energy and cooldown checks.
- Implemented logic for swapping two curses or repositioning a target, depending on the situation.

4. HAND-TRACKING MODELS AND GESTURES
-----------------------------------
- Reverted visible hand models to joint spheres only, removing the full skinned-hand appearance.
- Kept gesture recognition based on tracked finger joints.
- Added brief casting animations without intentionally changing tracked joint positions.
- Updated gesture instructions for supported characters and techniques.
- Changed Mahoraga's summoning gesture to make it easier to perform.

5. GOJO — LIMITLESS
-------------------
Hollow Purple:
- Increased damage from 85 to 140.
- Increased the hit radius.
- Increased cursed energy cost from 42 to 55.
- Increased cooldown from 14 to 20 seconds.

Red and Blue:
- Swapped gesture mappings:
  - Pointing activates Red.
  - A peace sign activates Blue.
- Added short charge-up animations and growing energy effects before launching the techniques.
- Updated gesture instructions to match.

6. MEGUMI — MAHORAGA
--------------------
- Initially reduced summoning time from 850 ms to 500 ms.
- Temporarily reduced cursed energy cost from 62 to 42 and cooldown from 24 to 16 seconds.
- After the correction, restored cost to 62 and cooldown to 24 seconds.
- Kept the shorter 500 ms summoning time.
- Replaced the original difficult gesture with bringing both open palms close together.
- Updated on-screen instructions.

7. DIFFICULTY AND PERFORMANCE
-----------------------------
- Made enemy health and speed scale more gradually between grades.
- Reduced how aggressively later encounters scale.
- Reduced rendering-resolution overhead to help performance.
- Added an enemy-spawning cap to limit excessive simultaneous enemies.
- Applied difficulty scaling to incoming damage.
- Kept separate student, normal, and special difficulty behavior.

8. OTHER TECHNIQUES AND EFFECTS
-------------------------------
The current game also includes technique effects and animations for:
- Gojo's Blue, Red, Hollow Purple, and Unlimited Void.
- Sukuna's Dismantle, Cleave, Malevolent Shrine, and Fuga-style fire arrow.
- Megumi's Divine Dogs, Nue, Toad, Mahoraga, and Chimera Shadow Garden.
- Jogo's Ember Insects, Flame Burst, and Maximum Meteor.
- Todo's close-range combo and Boogie Woogie.
- Hakari's Sliding Doors, Rush Wave, Shoulder Shockwave, and Idle Death Gamble.

These are features present in the current source; their exact visual quality and gameplay behavior should be verified in VR.

9. TESTING CHECKLIST / KNOWN ITEMS
----------------------------------
- Sliding Doors: Verify that it adjusts to the nearest enemy correctly at different distances.
- Gojo's gestures: Verify that Red and Blue are reliably distinguished and charge animations finish before attacks launch.
- Mahoraga: Verify that the two-open-palms gesture is easy to trigger without accidental summons.
- Todo: Verify that bringing both fists together activates Boogie Woogie without triggering the normal combo.
- Training: Verify that Quit Training returns cleanly to the mode-selection screen.
- Performance: Verify smooth gameplay during large encounters and Hakari's domain.
- Cooldown GUI: The cooldown GUI was previously reported as bugged. It still needs explicit verification; a fix has not been confirmed.

IMPORTANT
--------
This is a summary of requested changes and the implementation described for the latest source, not a claim that every feature has been fully playtested.
